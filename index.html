Questions people often ask

Is CivicShield free?

Yes. This demo has no sign-up and no cost.

What does the Safety Score mean?

70 and above looks safe, 40 to 69 is suspicious, and below 40 is dangerous. It is guidance, not a guarantee, so stay careful even with a high score.

Is my data kept private?

What you paste is sent to our server to be checked, and it is not stored. For links, the domain name is looked up with a public registration service, and with Google’s unsafe-sites list if that is switched on. If you report something, only a scrambled hash is saved. Your theme, text size and XP stay on your device.

Where do the scam patterns come from?

The Scam Database is an educational library of common scam types written for this project. It is not a feed of live user reports.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. The pages resize for phones and tablets.
